📦 About Tiger-Scale-V3

TigerScale V3 provides a connected solution for managing your 3D printing filament. It uses the TigerTag open NFC standard to track material usage. The hardware runs on an ESP32-S3 chip and features a 3.5-inch touchscreen for local control. Dual NFC readers identify your spool, while internal battery support ensures you can place the device anywhere in your workshop.

🚀 Getting Started

Follow these steps to set up the Tiger-Scale-V3 software on your Windows computer. This process prepares your device to communicate with your hardware scales and manage filament data.

Step 1: System Requirements

Ensure your computer meets these basic requirements before you begin:

  • Windows 10 or Windows 11 operating system.
  • A free USB port to connect your TigerScale hardware.
  • An active internet connection to download the necessary files.

⚙️ Connecting Your Hardware

After the software installs, connect your TigerScale device to your computer using a high-quality USB data cable. The application automatically detects the device. If the computer does not recognize the scale, ensure you have the proper USB drivers installed. The touchscreen interface on the device should light up, indicating that it has power and is ready for calibration.

🛠 Usage Tips

The TigerScale system works by reading NFC tags attached to your filament spools. Place a compatible TigerTag spool near the sensors on the scale. The device reads the spool information and updates the weight measurement on the screen. You can view detailed historical data through the computer application to track how much material you consume over time.

❓ Troubleshooting

If you encounter issues, try these steps:

  • Connection Errors: Unplug the USB cable and plug it back into a different port on your computer.
  • Software Crashes: Close the application completely and restart your computer to clear any background conflicts.
  • NFC Issues: Ensure your NFC tags are positioned directly over the sensor areas on the hardware unit.
